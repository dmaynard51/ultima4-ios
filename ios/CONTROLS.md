# Ultima IV on iOS — Touch Controls

The game runs at the classic 320×200, centred with the controls in the side
bars, in either **landscape** or **portrait**.

| Action | Touch |
|---|---|
| **Move** | On-screen **D-pad** (bottom-left) |
| **Commands** (talk, look, get, open, cast, ztats…) | Show the keyboard, then press the command letter |
| **Confirm / advance text** | Return on the keyboard, or the **↵** button (landscape) |
| **Cancel / pass turn** | Space on the keyboard, or the **Spc** button (landscape) |
| **Back out of a menu** | **Esc** button |

Ultima IV is driven almost entirely by single-letter commands — e.g. `t`alk,
`l`ook, `g`et, `o`pen, `j`immy, `z` (ztats), `c`ast, `b`oard, `e`nter — so the
**keyboard is your main tool**. Conversations are answered by typing keywords
(like `name`, `job`, `health`, `bye`).

In **landscape**, the keyboard is optional: tap **⌨** (bottom-right) to show
or hide it. When it's up, the game scales to stay fully visible above it, and
the buttons stay put; empty screen areas pass touches through to the game.

In **portrait**, the keyboard is always up (there's more room for it without
crowding the game view), so there's no ⌨ toggle, and Return/Space are just
the keyboard's own keys rather than separate buttons -- only D-pad and Esc
are shown, anchored just above the keyboard.

Your progress is saved automatically to the app's Documents folder.
